# Changelog

## v1.0.0 - 2026-10-06

- Published the first stable, versioned SmartHomeShop LD2450 package contract.
- Standardized shared LD2450 entities for UltimateSensor, UltimateSensor Mini
  and CeilSense product firmware.
- Included four polygon zones, two exclusion zones, two directional entry
  lines and persistent people counting.
- Added stable wall- and ceiling-mount metadata through the shared
  `radar-mounting` profiles.
- Ensured excluded mirror-reflection targets cannot update entry-line history
  or persistent people counts.
